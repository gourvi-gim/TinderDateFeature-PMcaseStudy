# Tinder DATE: An AI-Powered Date Planning Feature

A product management case study set in Tinder's Growth team. Many matches never turn into real dates, partly because couples struggle to decide **where to meet, where to eat and what to do**. This project designs a feature called **DATE** to solve that.

📄 **[View the full case study (PDF)](Tinder.date.project.pdf)**

## The problem
- In the deck's secondary research, only 16% of users had met a partner through dating apps, and around 50% never actually go on dates.
- Users waste time and often end up with repetitive, uninspired dates because they can't agree on a place, a cuisine or an activity.

## The solution
**DATE** is a new button inside the chat. Both matched users fill in their preferences for what to do, where to eat and where to meet. If both turn the feature on, an AI-driven recommendation engine cross-references their inputs and suggests date ideas with distance, ratings and booking options.

## Approach
- **User research:** personas, pain points and secondary research
- **Root-cause analysis** of the three core problems
- **Prioritization:** six candidate solutions scored with the RICE framework
- **Feature design:** user flow on Tinder and a high-level system design
- **Risks and mitigations**
- **Success metrics:** North Star (% of users using DATE), NPS and CSAT

## Key outcomes
- Top solutions by RICE score: Activity Matchmaker (216), Local Cuisine Explorer (136), Favorite Spots List (100)
- Long-term vision: turn Tinder from a matching app into an all-in-one date planning platform, with restaurant and venue partnerships
